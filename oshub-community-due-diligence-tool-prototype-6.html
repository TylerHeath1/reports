OS Hub Community Due Diligence Tool · an example of what's possible

What if the worker who makes t‑shirts for Amazon had the same visibility and power in supply chains as the company CEO, using that visibility to drive accountability from the ground up?

OS Hub is building the data and community infrastructure to make that a reality for the 450 million workers in global supply chains.

1The situation

A grievance surfaces.

Unpaid wages, unsafe conditions, harassment, retaliation for speaking up. Workers rarely take these to official channels. They tell the people they already trust: a paralegal at a legal aid centre, a union organizer, a community monitor.

→
2The problem

To act on it, they need to know who has power and what can influence them.

Who buys from the factory, which laws apply, which accountability channels exist, what worked elsewhere. Power sits in many places at once, which is why accountability has to be collective. Today that knowledge is scattered across thousands of disparate sources.

→
3What this changes

Accessible, actionable, collaborative infrastructure to power corporate accountability.

OS Hub brings it all together. Workers and their advocates stand on the same data and connections the biggest companies have, and act on them together.

Want to follow one specific campaign, start to finish? Aisha · Apex Garments · Pakistan Open ⌄Close ⌃
▌ The situation

Sumera, 26, a machine operator at Apex Garments Mills in Faisalabad, hasn't received overtime pay in three months. When she asked, her supervisor threatened her shifts. She told a paralegal she trusts — one of 5,082 worker reports logged through the MyVox community-monitoring program since 2022.

▌ The problem

Her report reaches Aisha Khan, paralegal lead at PILER. Aisha knows the violation is real. What she can't see is what she needs to act on it: who actually buys from Apex, which laws apply, which accountability channels exist, what worked elsewhere. That intelligence exists — scattered across registries and databases, or locked inside corporate platforms.

▌ What this changes

This workspace combines worker testimony with Open Supply Hub's open data — corporate chains, legislation, audits, contacts — and surfaces every accountability pathway at once. Affected workers vote on each step. Campaigns that took months to research start in hours.
