Ways of working
New meeting structure, tools, and coordination
Cross-collaborative projects now have a standard structure: one Slack channel + canvas as the front door, linked to a Drive folder, Sheet, Granola folder, and Claude project — all sharing the same name.
Where things live memo Training video (Slack thread)
Four recurring touchpoints now keep cross-functional work moving.
Product delivery meeting — the pipes: provides status on all roadmap items and surfaces dependencies so we can see exactly where the work flows and where it gets stuck
Data pipeline meeting — the water: brings programs and product together around the actual data moving through the org — what's flowing, what's blocked, what needs attention
Programs team meeting — updated to include broader team transparency; examples include community feedback sessions and programs reporting out on stakeholder engagement
Product, engineering & data — formerly separate team meetings, now merged into one shared touchpoint
