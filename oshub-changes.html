What's changing,
and why.

Two forces are shaping how we need to operate. Here's what's driving the change — and seven things we've done and are doing in response.

01
Teaming updates
How our teams are structured
New Programs team — previously mixed across Sales, Customer Success, and Stakeholder Engagement. Now a defined team with its own identity, cadence, and responsibilities.
Product, engineering & data merged — formerly working in separate lanes, now one building team moving together toward shared goals.
Org chart
02
Meeting updates
New and updated meeting structure across the org
Product delivery meeting — status on all roadmap projects, products, infrastructure, and services — surfaces dependencies and where things get stuck
Data pipeline meeting — brings programs and product together around the actual data moving through the org — what's flowing, what's blocked, what needs attention
Team meeting updates — Programs team and Product, Engineering & Data have new meeting structures specific to their teams
Meetings audit
03
Tooling & coordination updates
Where things live and how we work across tools
Each tool gets one job — not consolidation, but a clear defined use case for every tool we use, so people know where to find things and where to do work
Cross-collab project structure — every project gets the same containers (Slack channel + canvas as the front door, Drive folder, Sheet, Granola folder, Claude project), all sharing the same name — which makes it easier for Claude to search across tools

04
Product prioritization
How we decide what to build
We're shifting to a product-led, user-story-driven approach — not reactive to specific requests, but prioritized around user problems.
User stories — the foundation for how we define and prioritize work; what a user needs and desires is what shapes what we build
Roadmap — live source of truth for everyone to see what's being developed, its priority, and its status across all active work streams
