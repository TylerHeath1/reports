OS Hub Community Due Diligence Tool · an example of what's possible — not a finished product

What if the worker making t‑shirts for Amazon had the same information as Amazon's CEO?

Platforms like Altana and Sayari sell supply-chain intelligence to corporations. Open Supply Hub is the open data layer underneath — open to everyone, owned by no one — and this prototype shows what happens when that intelligence reaches the people closest to the problem, with workers owning and benefiting from the data they create.

▌ The situation

Sumera, 26, a machine operator at Apex Garments Mills in Faisalabad, hasn't received overtime pay in three months. When she asked, her supervisor threatened her shifts. She told a paralegal she trusts — one of 5,082 worker reports logged through the MyVox community-monitoring program since 2022.

▌ The problem

Her report reaches Aisha Khan, paralegal lead at PILER. Aisha knows the violation is real. What she can't see is what she needs to act on it: who actually buys from Apex, which laws apply, which accountability channels exist, what worked elsewhere. That intelligence exists — scattered across registries and databases, or locked inside corporate platforms.

▌ What this changes

This workspace combines worker testimony with Open Supply Hub's open data — corporate chains, legislation, audits, contacts — and surfaces every accountability pathway at once. Affected workers vote on each step. Campaigns that took months to research start in hours.

▌ The expected outcome PKR 4.26M in wages back to 347 workers Retaliation protection + 12 months monitoring Every campaign feeds the patterns that change the system
